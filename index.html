Our Operations

Premium Sunflower Oil Production

Refining Excellence

Our sunflower oil undergoes a meticulous refining process, including neutralization, bleaching, and deodorization. We prioritize nutrient fortification, ensuring every bottle meets the highest standards of Vitamin A enrichment for a healthier community.

  • Modern Refining: Advanced chemical refining stages.
  • Fortification: Integrated Vitamin A dosing systems.
  • Quality Control: Strict monitoring from seed to shelf.

Industrial Aquaculture

High-Density Fish Farming

Specializing in industrial-scale tilapia production using Recirculating Aquaculture Systems (RAS). Our facility is designed for maximum efficiency, managing thousands of fish per pond with optimized aeration and water quality control.

  • RAS Technology: Sustainable water recycling systems.
  • BSF Integration: Utilizing Black Soldier Fly larvae for high-protein, eco-friendly feed.
  • Scale: Large-scale commercial capacity.
